GLOBALBANK FINANCIAL SERVICES
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK 2024
Effective: January 1, 2024
HR Policy Owner: Sarah Chen, Chief People Officer
Document Reference: HR-POL-HANDBOOK-2024-v3.1

This handbook applies to all permanent employees of GlobalBank Financial
Services globally, unless otherwise stated. Specific local policies may
supplement or supersede this handbook where required by local law.

================================================================================
CHAPTER 1: EMPLOYMENT PRINCIPLES
================================================================================

1.1 AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT (US)
Employment at GlobalBank in the United States is at-will, meaning either
the employee or GlobalBank may terminate the employment relationship at any
time with or without cause, subject to the notice periods in Section 6.

1.2 CODE OF CONDUCT
All GlobalBank employees must complete the GlobalBank Code of Conduct
e-learning module within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter.
Failure to complete is a disciplinary matter and may result in system access
restrictions. The Code of Conduct covers: anti-bribery, conflicts of interest,
information security, market conduct, and client confidentiality.

1.3 FINRA AND REGULATORY OBLIGATIONS
Employees in roles requiring FINRA registration (Series 7, 63, 65, 79, etc.)
must maintain their registrations in good standing at all times. Any regulatory
inquiry, complaint, or outside business activity must be disclosed to
Compliance immediately. Failure to disclose is grounds for immediate termination.

CHAPTER 2: ANNUAL LEAVE AND HOLIDAYS
================================================================================

2.1 ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT

Annual leave entitlement is based on grade level:

  Grade 1 to Grade 8:    15 days per year
  Grade 9 to Grade 12:   20 days per year
  Grade 13 and above:    25 days per year

Annual leave accrues at the rate of 1.25 days per month (Grade 1-8),
1.67 days per month (Grade 9-12), or 2.08 days per month (Grade 13+),
beginning from the first day of employment.

New joiners receive a pro-rated entitlement based on their start date.

2.2 CARRY-OVER
A maximum of 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried over from one
calendar year to the next. Carried-over leave must be used by March 31 of
the following year or it will be forfeited. No monetary payout is provided
for forfeited leave except in exceptional circumstances approved by the CPO.

2.3 ANNUAL LEAVE APPROVAL
Requests for annual leave should be submitted in Workday with at least:
  - 2 weeks notice for leave of 1-5 days
  - 4 weeks notice for leave of 6-10 days
  - 8 weeks notice for leave of more than 10 days
Leave is subject to manager approval and operational requirements of the team.

2.4 HOLIDAY SCHEDULE (US)
GlobalBank observes the following US Federal Holidays (11 days per year):
  New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day,
  Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day,
  Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day.

Trading floor employees in Global Markets may be required to work on
public holidays if markets are open in other time zones. Holiday premium
pay of 1.5x will be applied for hours worked on a federal holiday.

CHAPTER 3: SICK LEAVE AND HEALTH
================================================================================

3.1 SICK LEAVE
All employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
Sick leave does not accrue and resets on January 1 each year.
Unused sick leave is not paid out upon termination.

For absences of more than 5 consecutive days due to illness, a medical
certificate from a licensed physician is required.

3.2 SERIOUS MEDICAL CONDITIONS
Employees with serious medical conditions (including cancer treatment,
major surgery, or mental health crises requiring hospitalisation) may
take unlimited paid sick leave with HR approval, subject to regular review.
This is in addition to FMLA entitlements (US employees).

3.3 OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH
GlobalBank provides access to an occupational health service for work-related
injuries and health assessments. Contact HR for referral.

================================================================================
CHAPTER 4: PARENTAL LEAVE
================================================================================

4.1 PRIMARY CAREGIVER LEAVE
The primary caregiver (regardless of gender) receives 20 weeks of fully
paid parental leave upon the birth, adoption, or fostering of a child.
Eligibility: Employees with at least 6 months continuous service.
Leave may begin up to 4 weeks before the expected date of birth/placement.
Payment: 100% of base salary during the 20-week period.

4.2 SECONDARY CAREGIVER LEAVE
The secondary caregiver receives 10 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Eligibility: Employees with at least 6 months continuous service.
This leave must be taken within the first 12 months of the child's birth
or placement.
Payment: 100% of base salary during the 10-week period.

4.3 RETURN TO WORK
Employees who take parental leave are required to return to work for a
minimum of 3 months following the end of their leave, or repay the leave
pay on a pro-rata basis.
Exceptions may be granted by the CPO in exceptional circumstances.

4.4 ADDITIONAL LEAVE
After the paid parental leave period, additional unpaid leave of up to
12 weeks may be taken with manager and HR approval.

CHAPTER 5: REMOTE WORK POLICY
================================================================================

5.1 HYBRID WORKING ARRANGEMENT
GlobalBank operates a hybrid working model. The standard arrangement is:
  Minimum 3 days in the office per week
  Maximum 2 days remote work per week

5.2 REGULATED ROLES
Due to Financial Services Authority (FCA) and Financial Industry Regulatory
Authority (FINRA) supervision and conduct obligations, employees in
regulated roles (investment banking, trading, sales, research) must:
  - Work from a GlobalBank approved office or home environment
  - Have a compliant home working setup (dedicated workspace, secure internet)
  - Be physically present in the office for client meetings and key supervisory events
  - NOT work from public locations (cafés, airports) unless using a VPN

Senior managers (Grade 11+) in regulated roles must be in the office at least
4 days per week due to supervisory responsibilities.

5.3 INTERNATIONAL REMOTE WORKING
Working remotely from outside your home country requires prior approval
from HR and Compliance. Tax and regulatory implications must be assessed.
Requests must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance.

5.4 HOME WORKING EQUIPMENT
GlobalBank provides a laptop and secure VPN access. Employees are responsible
for maintaining a suitable home working environment. A one-time home office
allowance of $500 is available to Grade 1-8 employees upon request.

CHAPTER 6: COMPENSATION AND BONUS
================================================================================

6.1 BASE SALARY REVIEW
Annual base salary reviews take place in March, effective April 1.
Reviews are based on individual performance rating and market positioning.
Employees rated "Below Expectations" (Rating 1) do not receive a merit increase.

6.2 ANNUAL PERFORMANCE BONUS
GlobalBank operates an annual discretionary bonus scheme. Bonus targets
as a percentage of base salary are as follows:

  Grade 1-3 (Analyst):         Target 10% of base salary    (max 20%)
  Grade 4-6 (Associate):       Target 15% of base salary    (max 30%)
  Grade 7-8 (VP):              Target 30% of base salary    (max 60%)
  Grade 9-10 (Director):       Target 50% of base salary    (max 100%)
  Grade 11-12 (Exec Director): Target 75% of base salary    (max 150%)
  Grade 13-15 (MD/Partner):    Target 100% of base salary   (max 200%)

Bonuses are paid in March following the performance year.
Actual bonus payments are entirely discretionary and may be zero in any year.
Bonus targets are subject to change at GlobalBank's sole discretion.

Employees must be employed on the bonus payment date to receive payment.
Employees who resign before the payment date forfeit their bonus entitlement.

6.3 DEFERRED COMPENSATION
Employees at Grade 12 and above are subject to mandatory deferral of 30%
of any annual bonus exceeding $100,000. Deferred awards vest after 3 years
and are subject to clawback provisions.
Deferral is into GlobalBank restricted stock units (RSUs) or a deferred
cash account, at the employee's election.

6.4 SIGN-ON BONUSES
Sign-on bonuses are subject to repayment if the employee leaves within 12 months
(Grade 1-8) or 24 months (Grade 9+) of their start date.

CHAPTER 7: BENEFITS SUMMARY
================================================================================

7.1 HEALTH INSURANCE
GlobalBank offers three medical plan options through Blue Cross Blue Shield:
  - HDHP (High-Deductible Health Plan with HSA)
  - PPO Core
  - PPO Premium

GlobalBank pays 80% of the monthly premium for the employee.
Dependent coverage is available; employee pays 100% of dependent premium.

Dental: Delta Dental (Basic and Major coverage included; Orthodontics available)
Vision: VSP ($150 frame allowance per 24 months; $25 exam copay)

7.2 HEALTH SAVINGS ACCOUNT (HSA)
Available to HDHP plan members only.
2024 IRS limits: $4,150 individual / $8,300 family
GlobalBank contributes: $500 per year (individual) / $1,000 (family) to HSA
Employee contributions are pre-tax via payroll deduction.

7.3 RETIREMENT – 401(k) PLAN
Plan provider: Fidelity Investments
Employee contribution: Up to 6% of base salary (traditional pre-tax or Roth)
Employer match: GlobalBank matches 50% of employee contributions up to 6%
  (maximum employer contribution = 3% of base salary)
Vesting schedule: 3-year cliff vesting for employer match
  (0% years 1-2; 100% from year 3)
Additional voluntary contributions allowed up to IRS limit ($23,000 for 2024)

7.4 LIFE INSURANCE
Basic: 4x annual base salary (fully employer-paid)
Supplemental: Employee may elect additional coverage up to 10x salary
  (subject to Evidence of Insurability above 4x)
AD&D: Equal to life insurance amount

7.5 DISABILITY INSURANCE
Short-term disability: 100% of base salary for 12 weeks
Long-term disability: 60% of base salary after 12-week elimination period
  (maximum benefit $25,000 per month)

7.6 EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAMME (EAP)
GlobalBank partners with Lyra Health to provide:
  - 6 free counselling sessions per year per employee (and immediate family)
  - Legal and financial advisory services
  - Work-life balance coaching

7.7 WELLBEING BENEFITS
  - Gym membership reimbursement: $75 per month
  - Headspace subscription: Provided free to all employees
  - Fertility treatment support: Up to $30,000 lifetime benefit

CHAPTER 8: GIFTS AND ENTERTAINMENT POLICY
================================================================================

8.1 GIFTS FROM THIRD PARTIES
Employees may accept gifts from clients, vendors, or counterparties up to
a maximum value of $75 per gift.
Gifts exceeding $75 in value must be:
  (a) Declined and returned, OR
  (b) Disclosed to Compliance and surrendered for charitable donation.

Cash gifts, gift cards, and gifts in kind of equivalent value are
treated the same as cash and may NOT be accepted in any amount.

8.2 GIFTS TO THIRD PARTIES
Employees may provide gifts to clients and business contacts up to $75.
All gifts to third parties must be:
  - For legitimate business purposes
  - Pre-approved by manager for amounts over $50
  - Recorded in the Gifts and Entertainment register (via Compliance portal)

8.3 ENTERTAINMENT
Business entertainment (meals, events, sports tickets) is permitted where:
  - It has a clear business purpose
  - It is not lavish or extravagant
  - The value does not exceed $250 per person per occasion
  - Expenses are submitted and coded correctly in the expense system

Entertainment events exceeding $250 per person or for more than 10 guests
require Director-level approval. All client entertainment must be
pre-approved by Compliance for employees in regulated roles.

CHAPTER 9: DISCIPLINARY AND GRIEVANCE PROCEDURES
================================================================================

9.1 DISCIPLINARY PROCESS
GlobalBank follows a progressive disciplinary process:
  Step 1: Verbal warning (documented)
  Step 2: First written warning
  Step 3: Final written warning
  Step 4: Dismissal

Gross misconduct (theft, fraud, harassment, serious regulatory breach)
may result in immediate dismissal without progressing through the steps.

9.2 NOTICE PERIODS UPON TERMINATION/RESIGNATION

  Grade 1-8:    2 weeks written notice
  Grade 9-12:   4 weeks written notice
  Grade 13+:    3 months written notice (or payment in lieu of notice)

9.3 GARDEN LEAVE
Employees at Grade 11 and above may be placed on garden leave during
their notice period, during which they are paid but prohibited from
accessing systems, contacting clients, or working elsewhere.

================================================================================
CHAPTER 10: DATA PROTECTION AND CONFIDENTIALITY
================================================================================

10.1 EMPLOYEE DATA
GlobalBank processes employee personal data in accordance with GDPR (UK/EU)
and applicable US state privacy laws (CCPA for California employees).
Employee data is used for HR, payroll, benefits, and regulatory compliance.

10.2 CONFIDENTIALITY
Employees must not share client data, deal information, or proprietary
GlobalBank information outside the firm. This obligation continues for
12 months after employment ends.
