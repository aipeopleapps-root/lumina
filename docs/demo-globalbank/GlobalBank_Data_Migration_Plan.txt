CRITICAL ISSUE DM-001: Cost Centre Mapping Gaps
  312 active employees have PeopleSoft cost centres that do not map to any
  active Oracle Financials cost centre. Root cause: 47 cost centres were
  retired in 2022 Oracle migration but employees never reassigned.
  Impact: Payroll GL posting will fail for these 312 employees at go-live.
  Owner: James Liu (VP Finance) and Rachel Torres (Director HR Ops)
  Deadline: Cost centre reassignments required by November 20, 2024.

CRITICAL ISSUE DM-002: Legacy Job Code Mapping Incomplete
  89 legacy PeopleSoft job codes (retired 2018 but still assigned to active
  workers) need to be mapped to the new Workday Job Profile structure.
  Current status: 47 of 89 resolved. 42 remain unresolved.
  Impact: 214 active employees cannot be loaded to Workday until resolved.
  These employees' managers cannot complete performance reviews.
  Owner: Rachel Torres (HR Ops) / GlobalBank HRIS team
  Deadline: November 12, 2024.

ISSUE DM-003: FINRA Licensing Data Incomplete
  253 employees in Investment Banking and Global Markets show as FINRA-licensed
  in PeopleSoft, but licensing details (Series type, license number, expiry date)
  are missing or incorrect in 12% of cases.
  Impact: Compliance reporting gap for licensed employees. FINRA requires
  accurate license data. Manual verification required with Compliance team.
  Owner: GlobalBank Compliance (Janet Morrison)
  Deadline: November 22, 2024.
