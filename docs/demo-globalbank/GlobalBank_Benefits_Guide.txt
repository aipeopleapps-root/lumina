SECTION 6: OPEN ENROLLMENT MIGRATION
================================================================================

CRITICAL ISSUE BEN-003: 2025 Open Enrollment Elections Must Be Migrated

Background:
  2025 Open Enrollment was conducted November 1–15, 2024 using the legacy
  PeopleSoft self-service portal and administered through the benefits
  administrator's portal (Businessolver). All 7,300 US employees made
  (or defaulted) their 2025 benefit elections in the legacy system.

  Since Workday go-live is December 2 (AFTER open enrollment), the 2025
  benefit elections must be loaded into Workday from the legacy system.
  This is a one-time data migration, not a standard OE process in Workday.

Migration Status:
  Total employees with 2025 elections: 7,300
  Elections migrated to Workday: 4,847 (66.4%)
  Remaining: 2,453 employees – elections NOT yet in Workday
  Target completion: November 28, 2024

Breakdown of remaining employees:
  Medical elections missing:         1,840 employees
  Dental elections missing:          2,100 employees
  Vision elections missing:          2,200 employees
  401k deferral elections missing:     890 employees
  FSA elections missing:               612 employees

  NOTE: An employee may appear in multiple categories above.

Risk of incomplete migration:
  Employees whose elections are not loaded by December 2 will appear in
  Workday as having NO benefits. The system will default them to no coverage.
  This means:
  a) Incorrect payroll deductions from first Workday paycheck
  b) Insurance carriers receive incorrect enrollment data
  c) Employees may not have coverage if they have medical events in December

  Owner: Jennifer Park (PM) + Lumina Benefits Lead
  CRITICAL DEADLINE: November 28, 2024
