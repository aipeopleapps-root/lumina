SECTION 1: COMPENSATION ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

GlobalBank uses a Grade-based compensation structure with 15 grades.
The grade architecture is designed to support investment banking and
financial services compensation practices including variable pay, bonus
plans, and deferred compensation programmes.

GRADE STRUCTURE:

Grade  Title                  US Base Range (USD)     Typical Population
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1      Analyst I               $65,000 – $80,000       Campus hires, 0-1 yr
2      Analyst II              $75,000 – $95,000       1-2 years experience
3      Analyst III             $90,000 – $115,000      2-3 years experience
4      Associate I             $110,000 – $140,000     MBA hires / 3-5 yrs
5      Associate II            $130,000 – $165,000     5-7 years experience
6      Associate III           $155,000 – $195,000     7-9 years experience
7      Vice President I        $190,000 – $240,000     9-12 years experience
8      Vice President II       $230,000 – $290,000     12-15 years experience
9      Director I              $280,000 – $360,000     Senior specialists
10     Director II             $340,000 – $430,000     Business leaders
11     Executive Director I    $410,000 – $520,000     Regional leaders
12     Executive Director II   $490,000 – $640,000     Senior business heads
13     Managing Director I     $600,000 – $780,000     Global business leaders
14     Managing Director II    $720,000 – $950,000     Divisional heads
15     Senior MD / Partner     $900,000 – $1,200,000   Most senior leaders

NOTE: The ranges above are BASE SALARY only. Total compensation for
Grade 9 and above typically includes a significant variable component.
Total compensation at Grade 13+ may be 2x–5x base salary when including
bonus, deferred stock, and other benefits.

CRITICAL RISK COMP-001: Market Data Currency
The compensation ranges above are based on Mercer Global Financial Services
Survey 2022 data. The data is now 2 years old. Compensation in financial
services, particularly for Grades 7–13, has increased significantly since
2022. Estimated market movement: 8–15% for front-office roles.
Recommendation: GlobalBank should commission a market data refresh before
merit review in March 2025. Risk of significant talent attrition in 2025
if ranges are not updated.

SECTION 2: MERIT REVIEW PROCESS
================================================================================

2.1 ANNUAL MERIT CYCLE

Annual merit review is conducted in January-March each year:
  January:    Managers receive compensation statements and guidelines
  February:   Managers submit merit recommendations in Workday
  March 1:    Compensation team reviews and calibrates
  March 15:   CPO and Division Heads approve
  April 1:    New salaries effective (first monthly payroll)
  March 31:   Bonus payments made (same month as merit increases)

2.2 MERIT BUDGET GUIDELINES (2024 cycle – for reference):
  Overall budget: 4.0% of total base salary
  Distribution guidance:
    Rating 5 (Outstanding):     6.0% – 9.0% merit increase
    Rating 4 (Exceeds):         4.0% – 6.0% merit increase
    Rating 3 (Meets):           2.5% – 4.0% merit increase
    Rating 2 (Developing):      0% – 2.0% merit increase (manager discretion)
    Rating 1 (Below Expectations): 0% (no increase, may be on PIP)

2.3 GRADE MINIMUM ENFORCEMENT
If a merit increase would bring an employee's salary below the grade minimum,
Workday will automatically flag this for HR review. Employees below grade
minimum receive additional adjustment to reach the minimum.

Award type: Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)
Vesting schedule: 3-year ratable (33.3% per year)
Performance condition: Relative TSR vs peer group (50% weighting)

SECTION 4: DEFERRED COMPENSATION
================================================================================

4.1 MANDATORY DEFERRAL POLICY

Applicable to: Grade 12 and above
Deferral rate: 30% of any bonus exceeding $100,000
Deferral vehicle: RSUs or deferred cash (employee election)
Vesting period: 3 years
Clawback: Awards subject to clawback for regulatory violations or
  gross misconduct for 5 years from award date

Estimated population affected: ~340 employees (Grade 12+)
