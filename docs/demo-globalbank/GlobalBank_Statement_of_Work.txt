2. SCOPE OF SERVICES
================================================================================

2.1 IN-SCOPE WORKDAY MODULES
- HCM Core (Organisations, Staffing, Talent, Absence)
- Payroll (United States – all states, Canada – Ontario and British Columbia)
- Benefits Administration (Medical, Dental, Vision, Life, 401k, HSA)
- Compensation Management (Base, Merit, Bonus, Deferred Compensation)
- Reporting & Analytics (Workday standard reports + 12 custom reports)
- Security (Role-Based Access Control, Segregation of Duties)

2.2 OUT OF SCOPE
- UK Payroll (734 employees) – Phase 2, timeline to be defined Q1 2025
- Workday Learning
- Workday Recruiting
- Bloomberg terminal integrations (client retains existing HR-Bloomberg feed)
- Integration with legacy fixed income trading system (APEX v4.2) – excluded by mutual agreement
- Historical payroll migration beyond 2 years (Jan 2022 onwards)
- Workday Adaptive Planning

2.3 INTEGRATIONS IN SCOPE
- ADP Tax Filing (outbound – replaces existing ADP connection)
- Fidelity 401(k) (outbound – census file, contribution changes)
- BCBS Benefits carrier connection (outbound – eligibility feed)
- Active Directory / Azure AD (inbound – new hire provisioning)
- Background check: First Advantage (inbound – adjudication status)
- Time tracking: Kronos WFC (inbound/outbound – time data for non-exempt)
- Payroll: Internal GL posting to Oracle Financials (outbound)

2.4 INTEGRATIONS EXCLUDED
- Bloomberg B-PIPE employee data feed (out of scope – separate agreement)
- APEX trading system HR data feed (out of scope – regulatory complexity)
