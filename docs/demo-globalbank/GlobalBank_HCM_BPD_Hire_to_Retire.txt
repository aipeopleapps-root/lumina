1. ORGANISATION DESIGN
================================================================================

1.1 SUPERVISORY ORGANISATION STRUCTURE

GlobalBank uses a Position Management staffing model. All positions must
exist before a hire can be initiated. Position Management is mandatory
because it enables headcount control, job requisition workflow, and
compensation grade enforcement.

Top-Level Org:     GlobalBank Financial Services (Company)
Second Level:      Business Divisions (6 divisions)
Third Level:       Departments (approx. 180 departments)
Fourth Level:      Teams (approx. 420 teams)

Maximum hierarchy depth: 4 levels below Company
Single supervisory organisation limit: 1 worker per org as manager
Cost Centre assignment: At the Department level (third level)

DIVISIONS:
1. Investment Banking (IB)
2. Global Markets & Trading
3. Asset Management
4. Consumer & Private Banking
5. Technology & Operations
6. Corporate Functions (Finance, Legal, HR, Risk, Compliance)

NOTE FOR CONFIGURATION: Investment Banking and Global Markets divisions
require additional security restrictions (Constrained Security Groups)
to prevent cross-division data visibility. Regulatory requirement under
information barrier (Chinese Wall) policy.

1.2 JOB ARCHITECTURE

GlobalBank has implemented a new job architecture as part of this project:
- Job Families: 24 (aligned to business function)
- Job Profiles: 312 (mapped from legacy 489 job codes – 177 consolidated)
- Job Grades: 15 (Grade 1 = Analyst, Grade 15 = Managing Director)

Grade Mapping:
  Grade 1-3:    Analyst
  Grade 4-6:    Associate
  Grade 7-8:    Vice President (Financial Services title convention)
  Grade 9-10:   Director
  Grade 11-12:  Executive Director
  Grade 13-14:  Managing Director
  Grade 15:     Partner / Senior Managing Director

NOTE: Financial Services grade titles differ from other industries.
"Vice President" at Grade 7-8 does NOT carry the approval authority
typically associated with the VP title in other sectors.

2. HIRE PROCESS
================================================================================

2.1 EXTERNAL HIRE WORKFLOW

Step 1: Job Requisition Creation
  - Initiated by Hiring Manager
  - Requires position to exist and be open (Position Management)
  - Grade 1-8 positions: Auto-approved after HR Recruiter review
  - Grade 9-12 positions: Requires Division Head approval
  - Grade 13+ positions: Requires CPO approval (Sarah Chen)
  - Target SLA: 2 business days for Grade 1-8; 5 days for Grade 9+

Step 2: Candidate Selection and Offer
  - Workday Recruiting is OUT OF SCOPE for this implementation
  - Offer initiation in Workday is triggered manually by HR once candidate
    selected in legacy ATS (iCIMS)
  - Offer Letter template: 3 standard templates (Permanent, Fixed-Term, Contractor)

Step 3: Background Check
  - Triggered automatically when offer status = "Accepted"
  - Integration with First Advantage for standard background check
  - Financial services enhanced check (credit check, FINRA BrokerCheck) for
    Grade 9+ and all roles with market access
  *** NOTE: First Advantage integration not yet in scope for FINRA BrokerCheck ***
  *** API access for BrokerCheck requires separate FINRA data agreement ***
  *** This is a GAP – manual process required for FINRA checks at go-live ***

Step 4: Hire in Workday
  - HR Coordinator initiates hire once background check cleared
  - Required fields: Legal name, DoB, SSN, address, I-9 documentation date
  - System auto-creates Workday account, triggers Azure AD provisioning
  - Equipment request triggered via ServiceNow integration (IT ticket)
  - Pay calculation effective Date 1 of next pay period after start date

Step 5: Onboarding
  - Day 0 (before start): Welcome email from Workday
  - Day 1: Manager pre-boarding checklist (IT setup, access, desk allocation)
  - Week 1: FINRA compliance orientation (mandatory for all employees)
  - Day 30: MANDATORY GlobalBank Code of Conduct e-learning completion
    (Regulatory requirement – tracked in Workday Learning – OUT OF SCOPE)
    *** RISK: Workday Learning is out of scope. Code of Conduct completion ***
    *** tracking will require manual Excel tracker until Phase 2.         ***
  - Day 90: Benefits enrollment deadline (90-day waiting period)

2.2 INTERNAL TRANSFER WORKFLOW (BPD-005)

  Transfer types handled:
  a) Lateral Transfer (same grade, different department)
  b) Promotion (grade increase)
  c) Demotion (grade decrease) – requires CHRO approval
  d) Inter-company Transfer (between legal entities, e.g. US to Canada)

  DECISION POINT (confirmed March 2024):
  Cross-border transfers (US to Canada or vice versa) require:
  - New hire in the receiving legal entity
  - Termination in the originating legal entity
  *** RISK: Cross-border transfers are very common in Investment Banking ***
  *** (estimated 30-50 per year). The separate terminate/hire process    ***
  *** will generate tax implications (final paycheck, PTO payout) that   ***
  *** are not yet documented. GlobalBank Legal to provide guidance.      ***

3. ABSENCE MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

3.1 ABSENCE PLAN CONFIGURATION

ANNUAL LEAVE:
  Grade 1-8:   15 days per year
  Grade 9-12:  20 days per year
  Grade 13+:   25 days per year
  Carry-over:  Maximum 5 days carry-over into new calendar year (Jan 1)
  Payout:      No payout on unused leave at year-end; terminated employees
               receive payout of accrued balance at termination

SICK LEAVE:
  All grades:  10 days per year (non-accruing, resets Jan 1)
  Serious medical: Unlimited (requires medical certification after 5 days)

PARENTAL LEAVE:
  Primary caregiver (birth, adoption):  20 weeks fully paid
  Secondary caregiver:                   10 weeks fully paid
  Eligibility: 6 months continuous employment
  Return to work: Employee must return for minimum 3 months or repay leave

COMPASSIONATE LEAVE: 5 days (immediate family), 3 days (extended family)

JURY DUTY: Full pay for duration of jury service (US only)

UNPAID LEAVE OF ABSENCE (LOA): Available for approved purposes, max 52 weeks

FMLA: Configured per US federal requirements (12 weeks unpaid, job-protected)

3.2 HOLIDAY CALENDARS

US Federal Holidays (all US employees): 11 days
New York additional: None (state holidays match federal)
Illinois additional: None
California additional: None
Texas additional: None

TRADING FLOORS NOTE: Employees in Global Markets may work on some public
holidays (markets may be open in other time zones). Holiday premium pay

4. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

4.1 ANNUAL PERFORMANCE REVIEW CYCLE

Timeline:
  Sept 1:     Self-evaluations open (all employees)
  Oct 15:     Self-evaluations due
  Oct 16:     Manager evaluations open
  Nov 15:     Manager evaluations due
  Nov 15-30:  Calibration sessions (by division)
  Dec 1:      Final ratings communicated to employees
  March:      Bonus payouts aligned to performance ratings

Rating Scale (5-point):
  5 – Outstanding       (top 5% of population)
  4 – Exceeds           (next 20%)
  3 – Meets             (target population: ~60%)
  2 – Developing        (10%)
  1 – Below Expectations (5%)

Forced distribution is a guideline, not a hard constraint in Workday.

5. TERMINATION / OFF-BOARDING
================================================================================

5.1 VOLUNTARY TERMINATION

Notice periods (by grade):
  Grade 1-8:    2 weeks written notice
  Grade 9-12:   4 weeks written notice
  Grade 13+:    3 months written notice (or pay in lieu)

Garden leave: Grade 11+ employees placed on garden leave during notice period
(paid, restricted from client contact and systems access)

TERMINATION CHECKLIST (Workday Tasks):
  - HR initiates termination in Workday (sets effective date, reason code)
  - Manager completes exit interview (optional)
  - IT access revocation: Immediate for sensitive systems (trading, email)
    via Azure AD integration
  - PTO balance payout: Automatic via payroll on final paycheck
  - Benefits termination: End of month in which termination falls (COBRA auto-triggered)
  - Equipment return: Tracked via ServiceNow

REGULATORY REQUIREMENT – FINRA DOCUMENTATION:
For employees who held FINRA-registered licenses (Series 7, 63, 65, etc.):
  - U5 filing required with FINRA within 30 days of termination
  - All performance reviews, complaints, and disciplinary records must be
    retained for 7 years (FINRA Rule 4511)
  *** RISK: Workday off-boarding workflow does NOT include a step to trigger ***
  *** FINRA U5 filing. This is a manual process. Must be documented as     ***
  *** a compliance gap and tracked manually until a solution is designed.  ***

5.2 INVOLUNTARY TERMINATION

  Requires: HR Business Partner approval + Legal sign-off for Grade 9+
  Severance: Calculated per UK/US/Canada Severance Policy (separate document)
  *** NOTE: Severance calculation is NOT automated in Workday for this impl ***

6. SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS (HCM)
================================================================================

INFORMATION BARRIERS (Chinese Walls):
  Investment Banking and Global Markets divisions require strict data separation.
  - IB HR Business Partners can only see IB employees
  - Markets HR Business Partners can only see Markets employees
  - Cross-division reporting requires CHRO approval
  - Configuration: Constrained Security Groups (worker-based security)

SENSITIVE WORKER DATA:
  - Compensation data: Restricted to HR BPs, HRDs, and Compensation team
  - Medical/health data: Restricted to Benefits team and employee only
  - FINRA licensing data: Restricted to Compliance and Legal

SEGREGATION OF DUTIES POLICY:
  - No single user may initiate AND approve a hire/termination
  - No single user may maintain payroll AND approve payroll
  - No single user may manage compensation AND process payroll
  SOD matrix approved April 5, 2024.

  Description: No automated trigger for FINRA U5 when registered rep terminates
  Risk: Manual process failure could result in FINRA regulatory action
  Workaround: Manual checklist maintained by Compliance team
