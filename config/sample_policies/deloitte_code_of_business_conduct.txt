DELOITTE AUSTRALIA
CODE OF BUSINESS CONDUCT
Policy Number: HR-002 | Effective Date: 1 January 2024 | Review Date: 31 December 2024
Owner: Ethics & Compliance | Approved by: CEO and Managing Partner

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. OUR COMMITMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Deloitte's reputation is built on integrity, professionalism, and trust.
Every employee is expected to conduct themselves with the highest ethical
standards in all work-related activities. This Code of Business Conduct
("Code") establishes the principles that guide our behaviour and decision-
making. Compliance with this Code is a condition of employment.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2.1 Definition

A conflict of interest arises when an employee's personal, financial, or
professional interests could influence — or appear to influence — their
ability to act in Deloitte's and clients' best interests.

2.2 Disclosure Obligation

All employees must proactively disclose any actual, potential, or perceived
conflict of interest to their manager and Ethics & Compliance as soon as they
become aware of it. Failure to disclose a conflict of interest is a serious
breach of this Code and may result in disciplinary action, including
termination of employment.

2.3 Common Examples

Conflicts of interest include, but are not limited to:
- Having a direct financial interest in a client or supplier organisation
- A close personal or family relationship with a client decision-maker
- Holding a directorship or advisory role at a competitor or client
- Receiving gifts or hospitality that could reasonably influence decision-making
- Conducting outside employment or consulting that conflicts with Deloitte work

2.4 Outside Employment

Employees wishing to undertake outside employment (paid or unpaid) must
obtain written approval from their Partner/Director before commencing.
Outside employment that competes with Deloitte, interferes with work
performance, or creates a conflict of interest will not be approved.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.1 Acceptable Gifts

Employees may accept gifts of token value (under $50 AUD) from clients or
suppliers provided the gift is not cash or cash equivalent, is infrequent,
and would not influence business decisions. All gifts received must be
recorded in the Gifts & Hospitality Register via the compliance portal within
5 business days of receipt.

3.2 Gifts Above the Threshold

Any gift with a value exceeding $50 AUD must be approved by the employee's
Partner/Director before acceptance and recorded in the register. Gifts
above $200 AUD must be declined or, where refusal would cause offence, donated
to a Deloitte-nominated charity.

3.3 Offering Gifts

When offering gifts or hospitality to clients, the total value must not
exceed $150 AUD per person per occasion and must be pre-approved by the
relevant engagement Partner.

3.4 Anti-Bribery

Deloitte has zero tolerance for bribery in any form. Employees must never
offer, give, request, or accept anything of value to improperly influence
a business decision by any individual, whether in the public or private sector.
This applies globally, regardless of local customs or practices.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INFORMATION SECURITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 Client Confidentiality

All information obtained about clients in the course of providing services
is strictly confidential. Employees must not disclose client information to
any third party without explicit written consent from the client, unless
required by applicable law. Client confidentiality obligations continue
after employment ends.

4.2 Deloitte Confidential Information

Proprietary methodologies, pricing models, internal financial data, strategic
plans, and personnel information are confidential to Deloitte and must not be
disclosed externally. Employees must exercise professional judgment when
sharing information internally on a need-to-know basis.

4.3 Data Privacy

Employees must comply with Deloitte's Data Privacy Policy and applicable
privacy legislation, including the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Personal data
must only be collected, used, and stored for legitimate business purposes.
Any suspected personal data breach must be reported to the Privacy Officer
within 24 hours of discovery.

4.4 Social Media

Employees must not post confidential client, engagement, or Deloitte
information on social media or public forums. Employees may discuss their
role at Deloitte in general terms but must exercise caution to ensure no
confidential information is inadvertently shared.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOUR
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 Respectful Workplace

All employees are entitled to a workplace free from harassment, bullying,
discrimination, and victimisation. Behaviour that undermines the dignity,
respect, or professional reputation of colleagues or clients is unacceptable
and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

5.2 Discrimination and Inclusion

Deloitte is committed to building a diverse and inclusive workplace. All
employment decisions, including hiring, promotion, and reward, are based
on merit and are free from discrimination based on age, gender, ethnicity,
disability, sexual orientation, religion, or any other protected attribute
under Australian law.

5.3 Workplace Health and Safety

All employees have a duty of care for their own safety and the safety of
those around them. Employees must comply with all WHS procedures, report
hazards or incidents immediately, and not attend work while impaired by
alcohol or other substances.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. USE OF FIRM RESOURCES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 Acceptable Use

Deloitte's systems, devices, and resources are provided for business use.
Incidental personal use is acceptable provided it does not interfere with work
performance, is not excessive, and does not involve inappropriate content.

6.2 Prohibited Uses

The following uses of Deloitte systems are strictly prohibited:
- Accessing, storing, or distributing offensive, discriminatory, or illegal content
- Circumventing security controls or accessing systems without authorisation
- Using Deloitte's name, logo, or systems for personal commercial activities
- Installing unauthorised software on Deloitte-owned devices

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. REPORTING CONCERNS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1 Speak Up Obligation

All employees have a responsibility to report suspected violations of this
Code, applicable laws, or Deloitte policies. Reports can be made to:
- The employee's manager or Partner
- People & Purpose (HR)
- Ethics & Compliance directly at ethics@deloitte.com.au
- The confidential Ethics Hotline: 1800 ETHICS (1800 384 427) — available 24/7

7.2 Non-Retaliation

Deloitte strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who, in good
faith, raises a concern, participates in an investigation, or declines to
participate in conduct that violates this Code. Any confirmed retaliation will
itself be treated as a serious disciplinary matter.

7.3 Investigation Process

All reports are treated confidentially to the extent practicable. Ethics &
Compliance will acknowledge receipt within 2 business days and complete
initial triage within 10 business days. Outcomes are communicated to the
reporting employee unless doing so would compromise the investigation.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. CONSEQUENCES OF VIOLATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action proportionate to
the severity of the breach, including formal warning, reduction of bonus,
demotion, suspension, or termination of employment. Serious breaches,
including fraud, bribery, or deliberate data misuse, will be referred to
the appropriate law enforcement authorities.
