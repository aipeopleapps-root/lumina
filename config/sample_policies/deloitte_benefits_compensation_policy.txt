DELOITTE AUSTRALIA
EMPLOYEE BENEFITS & COMPENSATION POLICY
Policy Number: HR-007 | Effective Date: 1 January 2024 | Review Date: 31 December 2024
Owner: Reward & Recognition | Approved by: Chief People Officer

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PURPOSE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy describes the standard benefits and compensation arrangements
available to Deloitte Australia employees. Benefits are designed to support
employee health, financial security, and professional development.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. SCOPE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy applies to all permanent employees. Some benefits are subject to
eligibility criteria noted within each section. Contractor and casual
arrangements are governed separately.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. REMUNERATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.1 Base Salary

All employees are paid a base salary expressed as an annual amount and paid
fortnightly in arrears. Salaries are reviewed annually in the April review
cycle. Merit increases are based on individual performance ratings, role level,
and market benchmarking data.

3.2 Superannuation

Deloitte contributes to superannuation in line with the Superannuation
Guarantee (SG) rate as set by the Australian Taxation Office. Effective from
1 July 2024, the SG rate is 11.5% of ordinary time earnings. Employees may
make additional voluntary contributions via salary sacrifice; contact Payroll
for the salary sacrifice agreement.

3.3 Annual Performance Bonus

All permanent employees who have completed a minimum of 6 months of service
by 31 March are eligible to participate in the annual bonus programme.
Bonus amounts are determined by:
- Individual performance rating (accounts for 60% of the bonus calculation)
- Business unit financial performance (30%)
- Firm-wide performance (10%)

Target bonus levels by grade are as follows:
- Analyst / Consultant: 5% – 8% of base salary
- Senior Consultant: 8% – 12% of base salary
- Manager: 12% – 18% of base salary
- Senior Manager: 18% – 25% of base salary
- Director / Partner: Governed by partnership agreement

Bonuses are discretionary and are not guaranteed. Bonuses are paid in July
following the financial year end. Employees who resign before the bonus
payment date are not entitled to a bonus payment.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. PRIVATE HEALTH INSURANCE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 Group Cover

Deloitte provides access to discounted private health insurance through the
firm's preferred provider, Medibank. Employees may elect hospital, extras,
or combined cover at preferential corporate rates, which are typically 5% to
12% below standard retail pricing.

4.2 Employee Cost

Private health insurance premiums are employee-funded and may be paid via
pre-tax salary packaging where eligible. Deloitte does not subsidise health
insurance premiums directly, however the group rates represent a material
discount to market pricing.

4.3 Enrolment

Employees wishing to enrol should contact Medibank directly using Deloitte's
corporate membership number, available on the intranet HR Benefits page.
Enrolment must occur within 60 days of commencement to avoid waiting periods
for pre-existing conditions.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. LIFE INSURANCE AND INCOME PROTECTION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 Group Life Insurance

All permanent employees are automatically enrolled in the firm's group life
insurance policy from their first day of employment. Coverage is equivalent
to 3 times the employee's annual base salary, payable as a lump sum upon
the employee's death or terminal illness diagnosis.

5.2 Total and Permanent Disability (TPD) Insurance

Group TPD insurance provides a benefit equivalent to 3 times annual base
salary upon diagnosis of total and permanent disability, as defined by the
policy terms. Coverage is automatic and at no cost to the employee.

5.3 Income Protection Insurance

Deloitte provides group income protection insurance that replaces 75% of the
employee's monthly base salary for up to 2 years in the event of an
extended illness or injury that prevents the employee from working. The
waiting period (elimination period) is 90 days. Coverage commences after
3 months of continuous service.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAMME (EAP)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

All employees and their immediate family members have access to the
Deloitte Employee Assistance Programme, provided by LifeWorks. The EAP
offers confidential counselling, coaching, and wellbeing support services.
Employees are entitled to up to 6 free counselling sessions per calendar
year. Sessions can be booked via the LifeWorks app, phone, or online portal.
All EAP services are strictly confidential; no information is shared with
Deloitte management.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT ALLOWANCE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1 Learning Budget

All permanent employees receive an annual professional development allowance
of $2,000 per calendar year for external learning activities. Eligible
expenditure includes: professional certifications, conference registrations,
relevant books, and online courses. The allowance resets on 1 January each
year and cannot be carried forward. Claims must be submitted via Workday
Expenses within 30 days of completing the activity.

7.2 Study Support

Employees pursuing an approved postgraduate qualification relevant to their
role may apply for study support through the Study Assistance Programme.
Approved employees receive up to $5,000 per year towards course fees and
up to 5 days of paid study leave per year for exams and intensive periods.
Applications must be submitted to People & Purpose for approval before
enrolment.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. PARENTAL LEAVE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 Primary Caregiver Leave

Permanent employees who are the primary caregiver of a newborn or newly
adopted child are entitled to 18 weeks of paid parental leave at full base
salary, subject to having completed 12 months of continuous service prior
to the expected birth or adoption date.

8.2 Secondary Caregiver Leave

Permanent employees who are the secondary caregiver are entitled to 4 weeks
of paid parental leave at full base salary.

8.3 Government Parental Leave Pay

Deloitte's paid parental leave is inclusive of any government-funded
Parental Leave Pay (PLP). Employees receiving PLP through Services Australia
are not entitled to receive both government PLP and Deloitte's full paid
parental leave simultaneously.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. SALARY PACKAGING
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Employees may access salary packaging for the following items, reducing their
taxable income where applicable:
- Superannuation (pre-tax contributions)
- Novated vehicle leasing (via the firm's fleet provider)
- Laptop and portable electronic devices used primarily for work
- Professional memberships and subscriptions

All salary packaging arrangements must be established through Payroll and are
subject to applicable FBT and ATO rules. Changes to salary packaging must be
submitted before the 15th of the preceding month to take effect in the
following pay cycle.
