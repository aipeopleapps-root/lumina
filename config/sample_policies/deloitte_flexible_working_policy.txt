DELOITTE AUSTRALIA
FLEXIBLE WORKING & REMOTE WORK POLICY
Policy Number: HR-004 | Effective Date: 1 January 2024 | Review Date: 31 December 2024
Owner: People & Purpose | Approved by: Chief People Officer

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PURPOSE AND PHILOSOPHY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Deloitte supports flexible working arrangements that enable employees to
deliver outstanding work for clients while maintaining their wellbeing.
This policy governs working from home (WFH), flexible hours, and hybrid
working arrangements. Flexibility is a privilege based on performance and
role suitability, not an entitlement.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. SCOPE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy applies to all permanent full-time and part-time employees.
It does not apply to graduate recruits in their first 12 months or to roles
designated as site-critical or client-on-site only.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. HYBRID WORK MODEL
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.1 Default Hybrid Arrangement

Eligible employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week, with a
minimum of 3 days per week in the office or at a client site. The specific
in-office days are agreed between the employee and their manager based on
team and client requirements.

3.2 Client-Facing Roles

Employees in client-facing roles must align their office/remote schedule with
client expectations. Where a client engagement requires full-time on-site
attendance, this requirement supersedes any personal hybrid arrangement for
the duration of the engagement.

3.3 Increased Remote Work

Employees wishing to work remotely for more than 2 days per week must submit
a formal Flexible Working Request via Workday. Such requests are reviewed
by the manager and approved at the Partner/Director level. Approval is based
on performance history, role requirements, and client impact.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. ELIGIBILITY FOR REMOTE WORK
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for remote work arrangements, employees must:
- Have completed a minimum of 6 months of service with Deloitte Australia
- Be rated "Meets Expectations" or above in their most recent performance review
- Have a suitable home workspace that meets the requirements in Section 5
- Not be subject to a current performance improvement plan

4.2 Probationary Employees

Employees on probation (typically the first 3 months of employment) are
required to work from the office full-time unless a medical or exceptional
circumstance is approved by People & Purpose.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. HOME WORKSPACE REQUIREMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 Minimum Requirements

Employees working from home must maintain a dedicated workspace that is:
- A private area with the ability to hold confidential conversations
- Ergonomically suitable (appropriate desk, chair, and monitor placement)
- Free from excessive noise and distractions during core hours
- Equipped with a stable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps download

5.2 Equipment Provision

Deloitte will provide all standard technology equipment (laptop, headset)
required for remote work. Employees are responsible for their own desk,
chair, and internet connection. A one-time home office allowance of $500
(ex-GST) is available to eligible permanent employees upon commencement.
This allowance must be claimed via Workday Expenses within 90 days of
eligibility. Receipts are required for all claims.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. CORE HOURS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 Core Availability Hours

All employees, regardless of work location, are expected to be available and
responsive during core hours of 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM local time. Outside core
hours, employees may work flexibly within agreement with their manager.

6.2 Meeting Attendance

Employees are expected to attend all scheduled meetings, whether virtual or
in-person, during their agreed working hours. Camera-on is the expected
standard for all internal and external video meetings unless otherwise agreed.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. INTERNET AND TELEPHONE EXPENSES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Deloitte does not reimburse ongoing internet or telephone service costs for
employees working from home. Employees should ensure their personal plans
are sufficient for their work requirements. Where mobile calls are incurred
for client-related work, these may be reimbursable in accordance with the
Expense Reimbursement Policy (HR-009).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. DATA SECURITY WHILE WORKING REMOTELY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 Required Security Measures

When working remotely, employees must:
- Use only Deloitte-issued equipment for work activities
- Connect to all Deloitte systems via the approved VPN client
- Never use public Wi-Fi (cafés, airports, hotels) without an active VPN
- Ensure screens are not visible to others in public or shared spaces
- Lock screens when stepping away from the workspace
- Report any suspected security incidents immediately to the IT Security team

8.2 Confidential Materials

Physical confidential documents must not be removed from Deloitte offices or
client sites. All sensitive information must be handled digitally and stored
on approved Deloitte systems only.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. PERFORMANCE AND ACCOUNTABILITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Remote working privileges may be reduced or revoked where:
- An employee's performance or productivity declines
- The employee is not responsive during agreed working hours
- Client or business requirements change
- The employee is not meeting the workspace or security requirements

Managers will provide reasonable notice (minimum 5 business days) before
changing a flexible working arrangement unless urgent business or security
reasons apply.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. INTERSTATE AND OVERSEAS WORK
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Working remotely from interstate for periods exceeding 4 consecutive weeks
requires approval from the employee's Partner/Director and notification to
People & Purpose due to payroll and tax implications. Working from overseas
is not permitted without formal approval from the CFO and General Counsel
due to tax, visa, and insurance implications.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
