DELOITTE AUSTRALIA
ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY
Policy Number: HR-001 | Effective Date: 1 January 2024 | Review Date: 31 December 2024
Owner: People & Purpose | Approved by: Chief People Officer

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PURPOSE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy sets out the annual leave entitlements for all Deloitte Australia
employees and establishes the processes for requesting, approving, and
managing annual leave.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. SCOPE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy applies to all permanent full-time and part-time employees of
Deloitte Australia. It does not apply to contractors, casual employees,
or secondees governed by separate arrangements.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. ENTITLEMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.1 Standard Annual Leave Entitlement

All permanent employees with less than 5 years of continuous service are
entitled to 20 days (4 weeks) of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual
leave accrues at the rate of 1.67 days per completed month of service.

Employees with 5 or more years of continuous service are entitled to 25 days
(5 weeks) of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing at 2.08 days per
completed month of service.

3.2 Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement calculated based on the
proportion of full-time hours worked. For example, an employee working 3 days
per week receives 60% of the full-time entitlement.

3.3 First Year of Employment

Employees in their first year of service accrue leave from their start date
and may access accrued leave after completing 6 months of continuous service,
subject to manager approval.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. CARRY-OVER AND EXPIRY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 Carry-Over Allowance

Employees may carry over a maximum of 10 days of unused annual leave from one
calendar year to the next. Any accrued leave in excess of 10 days at 31
December will be forfeited unless exceptional circumstances are approved in
writing by the employee's Partner/Director and People & Purpose.

4.2 High Leave Balances

Employees with annual leave balances exceeding 30 days will be required to
take leave within the following quarter. People & Purpose will notify
employees and managers when balances reach this threshold.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. REQUESTING ANNUAL LEAVE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 Notice Requirements

Employees must submit annual leave requests via Workday at least 10 business
days prior to the intended commencement of leave. For leave periods of 10
or more consecutive days, a minimum of 4 weeks' notice is required.

5.2 Approval Process

Leave requests must be approved by the employee's direct manager before
leave commences. Managers should respond to leave requests within 5 business
days. Managers may decline leave requests on reasonable business grounds,
including client commitments, project deadlines, or team coverage requirements.

5.3 Minimum Leave Period

The minimum period of annual leave that may be taken at any one time is
0.5 days (half day). Requests for leave periods shorter than 0.5 days will
not be approved except in exceptional circumstances.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Public holidays that fall during an employee's annual leave period are not
counted as annual leave days and will be credited back to the employee's
leave balance. The applicable public holidays are those for the state or
territory where the employee is primarily based.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. LEAVE DURING NOTICE PERIOD
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Annual leave may be taken during a notice period subject to manager approval.
Any accrued and unused annual leave outstanding at the termination date will
be paid out at the employee's base salary rate at the time of departure.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. ILLNESS DURING ANNUAL LEAVE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

If an employee becomes ill during a period of annual leave and the illness
extends for 3 or more consecutive days, the employee may request that the
period of illness be recredited to their annual leave balance and instead
recorded as personal/carer's leave. A medical certificate from a registered
practitioner is required for any such recrediting request.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. PAYMENT DURING ANNUAL LEAVE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Annual leave is paid at the employee's base salary rate. Allowances, bonuses,
and project-based payments are not included in the annual leave payment rate
unless required by applicable law or the National Employment Standards.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Managers are responsible for:
- Approving or declining leave requests within 5 business days
- Ensuring adequate team coverage is maintained at all times
- Monitoring team leave balances and encouraging employees to take regular leave
- Flagging excessive leave balances (>30 days) to People & Purpose
- Not pressuring employees to forgo their entitled leave
